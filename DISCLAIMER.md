# Research-use and safety notice

PsychRuleKit is research infrastructure. It is not a medical device, diagnostic
instrument, clinical decision-support product, or substitute for professional
assessment.

The engine reports whether supplied facts match a supplied rule set. A match is
not a diagnosis. Missing, ambiguous, culturally situated, developmental,
medical, substance-related, and longitudinal information can materially change
clinical interpretation.

No DSM-5 or DSM-5-TR diagnostic criteria text is distributed in this repository.
Anyone creating a diagnostic rule pack is responsible for permissions,
provenance, clinical review, validation, version control, jurisdiction-specific
coding, and regulatory compliance.

For safety-critical use, require independent clinical review, human oversight,
documented validation, and a fail-closed policy for missing information.
